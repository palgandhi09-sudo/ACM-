SC. 02 — The full story

About ACM SSGMCE

The ACM Student Chapter at SSGMCE is a prominent technical student organization affiliated with the world's largest computing society. Founded in 2005, we have served as a hub for computing enthusiasts to collaborate, learn, and grow for nearly two decades. We don't just talk about technology. We build it, compete with it, and ship it.

SC. 02 — The objective

Our Mission

Our mission is to enhance the coding culture at SSGMCE by fostering an environment of learning, sharing, and productivity. Guided by our motto — "Building technology. Building people." — we focus on both technical skill development and personal growth.

SC. 02 — The future

Our Vision

We envision a community where every individual has the resources, mentorship, and opportunities to tackle challenges in the field of computing, participate in global standards, and bring their most ambitious ideas to life.

LEGACY

The journey so far

2005

Founded

The ACM Student Chapter at SSGMCE was established, becoming a hub for computing enthusiasts to collaborate and learn.

2016

Inception Launched

Our flagship competitive programming contest began, setting the stage for algorithmic excellence.

2019

DotSlash Born

The inaugural 30-hour national-level hackathon, "DotSlash: Root to Ideas", was successfully organized.

2020

Pandemic Resilience

Transitioned to online contests like Inception 5.0 and the ACM Summer Challenge without missing a beat.

2026

Continuing the Legacy

Consistently hosting nationwide events like Epiphany, and staying true to our motto: 'Building technology. Building people.'
